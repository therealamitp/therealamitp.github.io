Research Overview

My research primarily focuses on computational biology, genomics, computational neuroscience, quantum computing, and cancer research. I have worked in four research settings spanning medicine, tumor evolution, neuroscience, genomics, and advanced computation.

I also connect my research interests with student leadership. I serve as president of IMSA’s Quantum Computing Club (Qubit), and serve as a student facilitator for the EnAct section of the LEAD program at IMSA.
